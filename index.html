Business challenge

Create a repeatable business-development process while keeping prospect selection, outreach and follow-up traceable. The same account can surface more than once, and outreach needs to reflect what has already happened.

My contribution

I designed and built the internal workflow around focused modules for collection, enrichment, qualification, draft preparation, CRM updates and monitoring. I placed review before consequential outreach and used explicit CRM state to coordinate follow-up.

Operating controls

  • People review prospect fit and the outreach draft. Sent activity is reconciled with CRM history before follow-up is prepared.

The workflow uses n8n, Baserow and Gmail. AI interprets company context and drafts recommendations. Deterministic logic handles scheduling, routing, validation and synchronization. I’ve used Salesforce hands-on in other projects. It is also a possible enterprise CRM integration for SDR-CRM.

Business challenge

Preparing each checklist manually repeated the same work across research, content, metadata and storefront setup. Batch production needed a consistent way to maintain editorial quality.

My contribution

I designed and implemented an AI-assisted pipeline that selects topics, prepares checklist content and product metadata in parallel, validates the output and hands approved items to Wix for publication.

Architecture

Diagram notation
  • Operational activity
  • External system
  • Mandatory human review
  • Decision

Operating controls

  • Track each checklist separately through a batch so that revisions and approvals are tied to the correct item.
  • Send incomplete or unapproved items through revision and review before publication.

Research inputs and AI services support preparation. Wix handles publication after editorial approval. I separated automated preparation time from review time so the comparison makes the remaining human effort visible.

Sketchsy Cinematic Pipeline

Cloud-based R&D for product films that preserve the original artwork through a 3D camera move.

Read case studyClose case study

Business challenge

A camera move through a reference interior has to preserve perspective, artwork proportions and the final crop. Cloud rendering also places limits on what can be tested in each iteration.

My contribution

I built and validated a camera geometry preview, then produced opening, frontal and final-framing stills to test composition and fidelity. I structured the workflow so camera and framing decisions can be checked before committing to a render.

Architecture

Diagram notation
  • Operational activity
  • External system
  • Mandatory human review
  • Decision
  • Primary workflow
  • Supporting dependency

Operating controls

  • Check that the camera remains aimed at the artwork during the curved move and aligns for the straight push-in.
  • Review proportions and framing against the original artwork before moving to render preparation.
  • Use intermediate stills and short previews to identify geometry and resource issues early.

Technology & design decisions

Blender supports scene reconstruction, camera validation and CPU rendering. Reference assets and original artwork serve as inputs. CPU rendering lets me validate the camera and composition within the available cloud resources.

Business impact

An accepted camera geometry preview and subsequent framing stills provide evidence of progress. The full production workflow remains active R&D.
